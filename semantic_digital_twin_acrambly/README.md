# semantic_digital_twin_acrambly

Semantic annotations of the objects of the ACRAMbly assembly tasks, the toy
aircraft and its parts and the tetris blocks, for the semantic digital twin,
together with their meshes.

## Meshes

The meshes are in `resources/meshes`, outside the Python source:

- `aircraft`: a surface mesh (`.stl`) per aircraft part, the COLLADA meshes
  of some parts, and the CAD sources of the wings in `Imported Components`.
- `tetris_block`: a surface mesh per tetris block, in millimetres.
